# 🛒 E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard** built using Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes sales, profit, customer orders, regional performance, and shipping costs to help understand business performance and identify opportunities for improvement.

The dashboard provides a clear view of important business metrics through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, tables, and filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare sales and profit across product categories.
- Understand regional sales performance.
- Analyze shipping costs and their impact on profitability.
- Identify important business insights.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- East is the strongest region in sales, profit, and total orders.
- Central is the second-largest regional contributor.
- Food and Disposables contribute strongly to sales and profit.
- The Other region has a very low contribution and needs further investigation.
- High shipping costs may reduce profitability in some product categories.
- Food and Supplements should be reviewed for shipping efficiency.
